Setups

Unlock to plan or log a trade.

Guide

This app has one job. It stops you taking a trade that breaks your own rules, and it remembers what happened when you did. No live prices, no orders. TradingView tells you a level was hit, Kite is where you trade, this is where you decide and record.

Plan · Sunday night

Enter every idea you charted. The big number is reward divided by risk. Green at 2 or better, amber below 2, red means the setup is broken and cannot be saved. The blue box gives you the share count for your capital.

Trigger
Where you enter.
Stop
Where the idea is proven wrong and you are out.
Target
Where you take profit.
What the target sits on
Name the level. If you cannot, it is a guess and the app says so. Four of your targets last week had nothing behind them.
Previous day range
High minus low, from the indicator already on your chart. Catches a stop sitting inside normal noise.
MIS or CNC
MIS is intraday with 5x buying power. CNC is delivery, no leverage, and shorting is impossible.

Everything you save starts as Pending. It goes nowhere near the Today tab until you mark it Confirmed, which you do after the Sunday review. That is how only the setups that survived the review reach your trading day.

Today · what it is for

This is the morning gate, and it exists because you wake up late and rushed. It answers four questions on one screen without you opening a chart.

How much can I still lose today
Your cap minus what is already committed. It stops a bad morning becoming a bad month.
Did it gap past my trigger
If price opened beyond your level the reward to risk you planned no longer exists. Tap Gapped past and it is dead for the day. This button saves you the most money.
Have I already traded
A second Taken is blocked unless you type a high conviction reason, which gets stored.
What is the news on this stock
Week prep notes sit under each setup, market overview above them. No sheet, no doc, no switching apps.

Log · after 3:30

Pick the setup, set the quantity you actually filled, enter both prices and say how it ended. The app computes Zerodha's real charges, brokerage, STT, exchange, SEBI, stamp duty and GST, and shows net profit after them. Gross profit is a lie at your size. A stopped out trade costs you roughly ₹40 more than the stop alone suggests.

Below that is your running profit and loss across every logged trade, and the count of rule breaks in the last 30 days. For the first month the rule break number is the one that matters.

The week

  1. Sunday, chart, enter setups here, review with Claude, mark the survivors Confirmed.
  2. Monday 2am, the prep task writes the news into this app on its own.
  3. Weekday, alert fires, check Today for a gap, place the order with a stop, tap Taken.
  4. After close, log it with the real quantity.
  5. Friday, clear the week in settings and delete the TradingView alerts.
