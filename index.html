Plan

This app has one job. It stops you taking a trade that does not fit your own rules. It does not show prices and it does not place orders. TradingView tells you when a level is hit, Kite is where you trade, this is where you decide.

Plan, Sunday night

Enter every idea you charted. The big number is reward divided by risk. Green is 2 or better, amber is under 2, red means the setup is broken. The bar under it shows risk and reward as actual widths so a bad ratio looks bad before you read it.

Trigger
The price at which you enter.
Stop
The price at which the idea is wrong and you get out.
Target
The price at which you take profit.
What is the target sitting on
Name the level. Previous day high, a swing, a resistance. If you cannot name one, the target is a guess and the app says so.
Previous day range
High minus low from the indicator already on your chart. Used to warn you when a stop is smaller than normal noise.
MIS or CNC
MIS is intraday with 5x buying power. CNC is delivery with no leverage and no shorts.

Shares and money at risk are worked out from your daily cap, ₹500 on ₹50,000. A red box means the setup cannot be saved. An amber box means it can, but only with a written reason that goes into the sheet.

Today, weekday morning

Shows how much risk you have left and every setup still live this week. Each one has three buttons.

Taken
You placed the order in Kite. Tap it straight after, not later.
Gapped past
Price opened beyond your trigger overnight. Do not chase, the R:R is already gone.
Skip today
You are not taking it today, it stays out of the way.

A second Taken in one day is blocked unless you type a high conviction reason. Below the setups you will see the week prep notes for each stock once the Sunday task has run.

Log, after 3:30

Pick the setup you took, enter both fills, say how it ended and tick any rule you broke. The app works out the profit and the R multiple and writes the row to your journal. The scorecard at the bottom counts rule breaks over the last 30 days. For the first month that number matters more than the money.

Settings, the gear

Backend URL, capital, daily risk percent, MIS leverage, minimum R:R, PIN, Face ID and the button that clears the week. Test connection tells you whether the phone can reach the backend.

The week

  1. Sunday. Chart, enter setups here, wait for the prep task, review with Claude, set TradingView alerts on the survivors.
  2. Weekday. Alert fires, check Today for a gap, place the order with a stop, tap Taken.
  3. After close. Log it.
  4. Friday. Clear the week in settings, delete the alerts.
